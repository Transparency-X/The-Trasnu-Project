# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment‑free environment for everyone involved in The Trasnú Project, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, immigration status, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We prioritise the safety and dignity of LGBTQ+ people, particularly those who are trans, of colour, disabled, or have precarious immigration status. Any behaviour that undermines that safety will not be tolerated.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use language that is respectful, affirming, and sensitive to trauma.
- Respect confidentiality: do not share any information about another participant’s identity, story, or status without their explicit, informed consent.
- Recognise and challenge your own biases.
- Give space to those with lived experience of the issues we study.
- Compensate community expertise fairly and transparently.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Deliberate misgendering or use of outdated/offensive terms.
- Disclosing another person’s sexual orientation, gender identity, or immigration status without consent (outing).
- Stalking, doxxing, or any form of online or offline threat.
- Sexual attention or advances where unwelcome.
- Dismissing or minimising someone’s experience of violence or systemic failure.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it by emailing `trasnu-conduct@example.com`. Reports will be handled confidentially by the project’s designated safeguarding lead. You can also use GitHub issues to report non‑urgent community concerns.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub repositories, online meetings, in‑person events, and private communications related to the project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, with additional provisions specific to the vulnerability of LGBTQ+ research participants and diaspora community members.
