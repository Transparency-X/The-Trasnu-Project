# Contributing to The Trasnú Project

Thank you for your interest in contributing. This project thrives on collaboration between researchers, frontline practitioners, community activists, and people with lived experience.

## Types of Contributions

- **Expert knowledge:** Offer your professional insight from housing, policing, immigration law, mental health, or human rights practice.
- **Community gatekeeping:** Help us reach LGBTQ+ individuals who have been forced to leave Ireland, NI, or Great Britain due to hostility.
- **Funding or fiscal sponsorship:** Support the project financially or by providing an institutional home.
- **Methodological input:** Suggest improvements to our ethical protocols, consultation frameworks, or data tools.
- **Advocacy amplification:** Help us place findings before governments, UN bodies, and the media.

## How to Get Involved

1. **Read our documentation:** Start with [`OVERVIEW.md`](docs/OVERVIEW.md) and the [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
2. **Open an issue:** If you have a suggestion, want to report a problem, or would like to propose a collaboration, open a GitHub issue. We use issues for all non‑confidential discussions.
3. **Email us confidentially:** For sensitive inquiries (especially if you are a potential lived‑experience participant or a gatekeeper organisation), please email `trasnu-project@example.com`. PGP key available on request.
4. **Join a thematic roundtable:** Invitation‑only roundtables will be announced. Express interest by opening an issue with the subject “Roundtable interest: [Your domain]”.

## Community Partnership Principles

- **We do not extract data.** All collaboration is co‑designed, and partner organisations retain full control over any information shared.
- **We pay for expertise.** Community partners and lived‑experience advisors are compensated fairly.
- **We prioritise safety.** If you serve a population at risk of immigration enforcement or family/community violence, we will work with you to design a completely safe data‑sharing pathway, including a “no names, no paper trail” option.

## Development & Repository Governance

- This repository is currently maintained by the project lead.
- All substantive changes to methodology or ethical protocols must be reviewed by the Advisory Group before being merged into the main branch.
- We follow a [Contributor Covenant](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) code of conduct.

## Contact

For any query not suitable for a public issue, email `trasnu-project@example.com`.
