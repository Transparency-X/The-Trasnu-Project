# Features

## 1. Multi‑Jurisdictional Consultation Framework
A phased, trauma‑informed blueprint for engaging stakeholders across Ireland, Northern Ireland, Great Britain, and global bodies, with distinct engagement methods for each phase.

## 2. Lived‑Experience Co‑Design Model
Compensated, confidential panels of directly impacted LGBTQ+ individuals who shape every research question, output, and recommendation.

## 3. Transnational Displacement Pathway Mapping
A conceptual tool to visualise how legal status (non‑EEA, EEA, dual citizen) determines forced migration routes and destination risks, using the Brazilian corridor as a template.

## 4. Composite Persona Methodology
Ethically safe, evidence‑based composite cases (e.g. “Rafael”, “Carla”, “Luana”) that embody real patterns without exposing any individual. Used to drive policy roundtables and training.

## 5. Practitioner Wisdom Interview Protocol
A standardised instrument to gather anonymised insights from frontline workers in housing, policing, mental health, and migrant support on the hostility‑displacement chain.

## 6. Policy Gap Analysis Matrix
A cross‑referenced assessment of where domestic and international law fail to protect against displacement‑inducing harassment. Covers hate crime legislation, housing law, employment protections, and asylum frameworks.

## 7. Ethical Data Protection Toolkit
Ready‑to‑deploy consent forms, data storage protocols, and mandatory reporting guidelines specifically designed for research with populations that include undocumented or precarious status migrants.

## 8. Global Advocacy Submission Pack
Templated submissions for UN Universal Periodic Review, Inter‑American Commission on Human Rights hearings, and UN Special Procedures, allowing rapid evidence‑to‑advocacy turnaround.

## 9. Open‑Access Knowledge Hub
All non‑confidential outputs (policy briefs, methodology guides, anonymised data summaries) published under a Creative Commons license to support global replication.

## 10. Replicable Corridor Framework
A documented, modifiable toolkit enabling adaptation of the whole approach to other diaspora corridors, such as Nigeria‑UK, Pakistan‑Ireland, or Ukraine‑EU routes.
