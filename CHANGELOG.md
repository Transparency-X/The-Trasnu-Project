## [1.0.0] – YYYY-MM-DD

### Added
- Initial public release of The Trasnú Project repository.
- `README.md` with project overview, aims, and repository structure.
- `docs/OVERVIEW.md`: extended problem statement, causal chain framing, and jurisdictional scope.
- `docs/COMMUNICATION_AND_CONSULTATION_STRATEGY.md`: complete multi‑phase stakeholder engagement plan including transnational corridor analysis and Brazilian case study hooks.
- `docs/RESEARCH_ETHICS.md`: trauma‑informed ethical protocol, informed consent pathways, data storage safeguards, and protections for participants with precarious immigration status.
- `docs/STAKEHOLDER_MAP.md`: detailed catalogue of 60+ organisations across all domains and jurisdictions, with website and email contacts.
- `docs/FEATURES.md`: catalogue of methodological tools and planned outputs.
- `docs/ROADMAP.md`: four‑phase roadmap (Foundation, Data Collection, Analysis & Outputs, Growth & Replication).
- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`: adapted Contributor Covenant with specific protections for LGBTQ+ and diaspora community members.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`: collaboration guide, partnership principles, and contact routes.
- Repository directory scaffold for `research/`, `outputs/`, `data/`, and `assets/`.
